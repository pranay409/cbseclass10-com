CBSE Class 10 Board Exam Strategy: Last 90 Days Study Plan That Works

Published: October 31, 2026

The final 90 days before your CBSE Class 10 board exams are crucial. With proper strategy and disciplined execution, this period can transform your preparation and significantly boost your score. This comprehensive guide provides a battle-tested 90-day study plan specifically designed for CBSE Class 10 students, grounded in NCERT curriculum and actual board exam patterns.

Understanding the CBSE Class 10 Exam Structure

Before diving into the 90-day plan, it's essential to understand what you're preparing for. CBSE Class 10 board exams are summative assessments that test your understanding of NCERT curriculum across all subjects.

Exam Pattern Overview

  • Duration: 3 hours per subject
  • Total Marks: 100 marks per subject (80 marks theory + 20 marks internal assessment)
  • Question Types: MCQs, Short Answer (SA), and Long Answer (LA) questions
  • Core Subjects: English, Hindi/Regional Language, Mathematics, Science, Social Science
  • Exam Conduct: Follows NCERT textbooks strictly with focus on application and conceptual clarity

Understanding this structure helps you allocate time effectively across subjects and topics. The new pattern emphasizes conceptual understanding over rote memorization.

Phase 1: Days 1-30 - Complete Revision and Concept Clarity

The first month is about thorough revision of all NCERT chapters across all subjects. This phase builds the foundation for targeted practice in the remaining 60 days.

Weekly Structure (Days 1-30)

Week 1-2 (Days 1-14): Focus on Science and Mathematics - These subjects require conceptual clarity and practice. Complete revision of NCERT Science chapters (Physics, Chemistry, Biology) and Math chapters from Class 10 NCERT.

Week 3-4 (Days 15-30): Focus on Social Science and Languages - Complete revision of NCERT History, Geography, Civics, and Economics chapters. For English and Hindi, revise grammar, literature, and writing skills from NCERT.

Daily Study Plan (Phase 1)

  • Dedicate 2-3 hours per subject each day
  • Read NCERT textbook chapters carefully, making notes
  • Highlight key definitions, formulas, and important concepts
  • Create mind maps for complex topics
  • Solve NCERT end-chapter exercises without time pressure
  • Review previous day's notes for 15-20 minutes

During this phase, aim for 100% chapter coverage across all subjects. Don't rush—understanding is more important than speed at this stage.

Phase 2: Days 31-60 - Practice and Pattern Recognition

With concepts clear, the next 30 days focus on solving practice problems and recognizing exam patterns. This phase builds problem-solving speed and accuracy.

Subject-Wise Strategy

Mathematics

Solve previous 10 years' CBSE board question papers (available in NCERT archives). Focus on:

  • Algebra and geometry from Class 9-10 NCERT
  • Trigonometry and statistics applications
  • Time your solutions: 80 marks in 3 hours = ~2.25 minutes per mark

Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)

Each science section follows NCERT curriculum closely:

  • Physics: Electricity, Magnetism, Light, Motion - solve numerical problems repeatedly
  • Chemistry: Chemical reactions, periodic table, equations - practice balancing and understanding reactions
  • Biology: Life processes, heredity, ecosystems - make diagrams (photosynthesis, respiration, cell division)

Social Science

Revise NCERT chapters 1-22 across History, Geography, Civics, and Economics. Create timeline for historical events and maps for geography.

Languages (English & Hindi)

Practice reading comprehension, writing essays, and letter-writing based on CBSE board guidelines. Solve previous year papers for pattern recognition.

⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid

Many students solve only 1-2 years of previous papers and assume they're prepared. You must solve minimum 10 years of CBSE board papers to recognize recurring question patterns and difficulty levels. Repeat solving papers 2-3 times with increasing time constraints.

Phase 3: Days 61-90 - Mock Tests and Strategy Refinement

The final month determines your actual board exam performance. This phase focuses on full-length mock tests, error analysis, and time management strategy.

Weekly Schedule (Days 61-90)

3 full-length mock tests per week - one per subject (or combined if you have access to quality mock test papers). Each mock should simulate actual board exam conditions:

  • Take mock tests in a quiet environment with no distractions
  • Follow strict time limits (3 hours per subject)
  • Don't refer to books or notes during the test
  • Mark answers according to CBSE marking scheme
  • Analyze wrong answers and note problem areas

Post-Mock Analysis (Critical Step)

After each mock test, spend 1-2 hours analyzing:

  • Which question types did you miss? (MCQs, SAs, LAs)
  • Which topics need revision? (Directly correlate with NCERT chapters)
  • Did you manage time properly? (Adjust strategy for longer/shorter answers)
  • What's your current score trend? (Should show 5-10% weekly improvement)

Last 2 Weeks Strategy

Reduce new practice and increase revision. These days are for consolidation:

  • Revise all formulas in Mathematics and Science
  • Re-read important chapters from NCERT (especially frequently asked topics)
  • Practice writing answers neatly under time constraints
  • Solve 2-3 final mock tests to build confidence
  • Sleep well - your brain needs rest to consolidate learning
