Triangles for CBSE Class 10: Similarity Theorems with Proofs

Published on November 22, 2026

Triangles form one of the most fundamental and scoring chapters in CBSE Class 10 Mathematics. Chapter 6 of the NCERT syllabus focuses extensively on the properties of similar triangles and the criteria for similarity. Understanding similarity theorems is not just essential for board exams but also forms the foundation for higher mathematics. This comprehensive guide covers all the major similarity theorems with detailed proofs and exam-focused insights.

What is Triangle Similarity? (NCERT Chapter 6 Introduction)

Two triangles are said to be similar if their corresponding angles are equal and their corresponding sides are proportional. Triangle similarity is denoted by the symbol ~ (tilde). Unlike congruence, similar triangles need not be of the same size but must have the same shape.

In CBSE Class 10, you are expected to know three main criteria for triangle similarity: AA (Angle-Angle), SSS (Side-Side-Side), and SAS (Side-Angle-Side). These theorems are frequently asked in both 2-mark and 4-mark questions on board exams. Mastering these will help you solve complex geometry problems with confidence.

⚡ Key Concept

Remember: If two triangles are similar (△ABC ~ △PQR), then not only are their angles equal, but the ratios of their corresponding sides are also equal. This proportionality property is the cornerstone of similarity problems and is often tested in combination with the basic proportionality theorem (Thales' theorem).

Similarity Criteria with Complete Proofs

1. AA (Angle-Angle) Criterion for Similarity

Statement: If two angles of one triangle are respectively equal to two angles of another triangle, then the two triangles are similar.

Theorem (NCERT 6.3): If in triangles ABC and PQR, ∠A = ∠P and ∠B = ∠Q, then △ABC ~ △PQR.

Proof:

Given: △ABC and △PQR where ∠A = ∠P and ∠B = ∠Q

To Prove: △ABC ~ △PQR

Proof:

  • Since ∠A = ∠P and ∠B = ∠Q, we have ∠C = ∠R (since sum of angles in a triangle is 180°)
  • Now we need to prove that corresponding sides are proportional: AB/PQ = BC/QR = AC/PR
  • Construct point D on AB and point E on AC such that BD = PQ and ∠ADE = ∠PQR
  • By ASA criterion, △ADE ≅ △PQR, which gives us AD/PQ = AE/QR = DE/QR
  • Therefore, AB/PQ = AC/PR = BC/QR, proving △ABC ~ △PQR

2. SSS (Side-Side-Side) Criterion for Similarity

Statement: If the sides of one triangle are proportional to the sides of another triangle, then the triangles are similar.

Theorem (NCERT 6.4): If in triangles ABC and PQR, AB/PQ = BC/QR = AC/PR, then △ABC ~ △PQR.

Proof:

Given: △ABC and △PQR where AB/PQ = BC/QR = AC/PR = k (say)

To Prove: △ABC ~ △PQR

Proof:

  • Construct a triangle DEF on side AB such that DE = PQ, ∠ADE = ∠P, and ∠AED = ∠Q
  • By AA criterion, △ADE ~ △PQR
  • Therefore, AD/PQ = AE/QR = DE/QR and AD = AB/k, AE = AC/k, DE = BC/k
  • Now, in triangles ABC and ADE: AB = AD (by construction), AC = AE (by construction), BC = DE (by construction)
  • By SSS congruence, △ABC ≅ △ADE
  • Since △ADE ~ △PQR and △ABC ≅ △ADE, we have △ABC ~ △PQR
