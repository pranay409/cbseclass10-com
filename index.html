Subject Strategy

Where Your Marks Actually Come From in Class 10

Most students spread effort equally across subjects. The smarter approach is knowing where easy marks are and making sure you do not drop them.

🔢

Mathematics

Completely scoring if practiced consistently. Chapters like Arithmetic Progressions, Triangles, and Statistics appear every year with predictable question types. Do not memorise, understand the derivations.

Key tip: Practice past five years' board papers. The pattern is very stable.
⚗️

Science

Split across Physics, Chemistry, and Biology. Life Processes and Light are the safest bets. Diagrams carry marks directly, especially in Biology. Do not skip the circuit and ray diagrams in Physics.

Key tip: Draw every diagram at least five times before the exam.
🗺️

Social Science

History, Geography, Civics, and Economics together. Map work carries 5 marks and is fully predictable if you prepare the standard NCERT maps. Long answers need structured writing, not just content.

Key tip: Answer long questions in points with underlined headings for better marks.
✍️

English

Grammar, writing tasks, and literature. The grammar section is fully rulebook-based and should give you full marks with a few weeks of practice. Literature answers that quote from the text score higher than generic answers.

Key tip: Write formal letters and essays weekly starting three months before.
